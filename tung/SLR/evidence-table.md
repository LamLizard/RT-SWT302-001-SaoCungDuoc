**Thẩm định:** Construct validity: IV-B pp.24745-24746 defines an occurrence by fault type/parameter/operation, not root cause; one defect may produce many occurrences, so 24372 cannot estimate unique mutants or independent bugs. Measurement: IV-A p.24745 groups399901 responses, investigates only68372 suspicious responses and treats others optimistically; V pp.24751-24752 mentions expert double checks but incomplete specs and subjective classification remain, leaving unknown false-negative/error rates. Internal validity: III-B/IV-A random valid workload feeds fault injection; it is not a separate matched-budget uniform-random arm. No causal EP/BVA advantage can be estimated. Dependence/repeatability: IV-A FL_REP3 and IV-C/V mean2.8 matching outcomes support short-run repeatability; repeated faults share service/operation/parameter and do not form independent trials or a confidence interval. External validity: heterogeneous52 services are a strength; purposive service selection, unsupported payloads424 operations and live concurrent traffic constrain transfer to45 RESTestBench requirements. Reporting: introduction24373 versus results/plotting data24372; setup399901 versus threats399001; method57 faults versus results49. Preserve inconsistencies; Figure-3 image and full result-table layout not visually checked. Remedy for this RQ: validate request boundaries against requirements/schema, pair equal request budgets by requirement, record random seeds/repeats and count unique oracle-confirmed mutant IDs; source-data recurrence is insufficient. Inferential effect sizes or confidence intervals for the target comparison are not reported.

**Kết luận được hỗ trợ:** In the selected APIs, schema-based black-box robustness testing with boundary/invalid-input rules disclosed observable robustness failures. This supports candidate input rules and manual-oracle procedures. It does not show EP+BVA outperforms equal-budget uniform random on RESTestBench unique-mutant detection.

**Thẩm định:** Boundary validity: Figshare GPTThree/Prompt_1.java lines18-32 selects1 and Integer.MAX_VALUE for petId, establishing attempted BVA. swagger.json lines60-67 says integer/int64 with no minimum/maximum: lower bound1 is unverified and int32 maximum is not a justified int64/API maximum; eligibility is distinct from correctness/completeness of BVA.
